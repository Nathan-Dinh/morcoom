# Healthcare Document Intelligence & Governance Platform

## Project Overview

The Healthcare Document Intelligence & Governance Platform is a multi-agent system designed to automate healthcare document processing through a persistent, hierarchically governed AI organization.

The platform combines document intelligence, workflow automation, healthcare interoperability, and organizational governance.

Rather than treating AI agents as isolated tools executing predefined tasks, the system organizes them into specialized departments with defined responsibilities, authority, and decision-making boundaries.

The organization operates through four interconnected architectural layers:

- Governance
- Administration
- Operations
- Organizational Memory

The architecture follows the Hierarchical Agentic AI Governance Architecture (HAAGA), introducing formal decision rights, escalation procedures, institutional continuity, and auditable accountability.

## Problem Statement

Healthcare organizations process large volumes of documents from external providers, laboratories, specialists, and patients.

Manual document processing creates administrative overhead, inconsistent data handling, delays, and limited visibility into operational workflows.

Introducing AI automation creates additional challenges:

- Agents may make decisions outside their intended responsibilities.
- Multiple agents may disagree about the correct outcome.
- AI-generated information may contain errors.
- Decisions may lack sufficient evidence or traceability.
- Organizational knowledge can disappear when agents or models are replaced.
- Automated workflows may operate without sufficient oversight.

The platform addresses these challenges by combining intelligent document processing with a formal organizational governance system.

## Proposed Solution

Build a persistent AI organization that manages healthcare document processing through specialized departments.

The system receives documents, extracts information, classifies content, matches patient records, and routes documents through configurable workflows.

AI agents perform specialized activities within formally assigned authority and competence boundaries.

When a decision exceeds an agent's authority, involves insufficient evidence, or produces unresolved departmental disagreement, it is escalated through the organizational hierarchy.

All recommendations, evidence, decisions, and outcomes are preserved in Organizational Memory.

## Long-Term Objective

The long-term objective is to demonstrate how autonomous AI organizations can operate persistent healthcare administration workflows while maintaining formal governance, institutional continuity, and accountability.

The platform will serve as a prototype for applying hierarchical agentic governance to healthcare technology environments.
